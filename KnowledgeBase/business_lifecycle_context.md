## 💰 Financial & Administrative Lifecycle

The business follows a rigorous administrative cycle:
1.  **Lead Generation:** Managed via `Kontak Iklan FB` and `Kontak CSV`.
2.  **Sales Proposal:** Detailed drafting of proposals (e.g., `D:\2026 Dilla\Proposal`).
3.  **Contracting:** Management of `SPK` (Surat Perintah Kerja) and `Draft MOU`.
4.  **Execution:** Delivery of training and managing `Jadwal Pelatihan`.
5.  **Certification:** Handling `LSP` requirements and `Hasil Ujian`.
6.  **Financial Settlement:** Managing `Invoice` and `Dokumen Pajak (BUPOT)`.
