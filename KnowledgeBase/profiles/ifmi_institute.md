# 🎓 Profile: International Financial Modeling Institute (IFMI)

**Role:** Independent Global Certification Body
**Standard:** Adheres to ISO/IEC 17024

## 🎯 Mission
To advance the fields of financial modeling, risk analysis, and performance planning through education, certification, research, and knowledge sharing.

## 📜 Certification Programs
IFMI provides professional certifications that validate practical competence for analysts and risk professionals:

1. **Professional Financial Modeler (PFM):** Flagship certification for advanced spreadsheet-based financial modeling, valuation, and forecasting. Accredited by IAS (USA) and KAN (Indonesia).
2. **Financial Risk Analyst for Corporation (FRAC):** Focuses on managing and analyzing corporate financial risk.
3. **Professional Project Analyst (PPA):** Validates competence in project feasibility, investment evaluation, and risk assessment.

## ⚙️ Operating Model
- **Impartiality:** IFMI acts as the certification body (examining and certifying).
- **Registered Training Providers (RTP):** IFMI does **not** conduct the training itself. It recognizes and audits RTPs (such as PT Rajawali Konsulindo) who deliver the actual training courses to students.
