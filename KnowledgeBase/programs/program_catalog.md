# 🎓 Program Catalog: PT Rajawali Konsulindo (RJ Consulting)

This document details the professional training and certification programs offered by RJ Consulting, focused on corporate finance and risk management.

---

## 🏆 Flagship Certification Programs

### 1. Professional Financial Modeler (PFM)
**Standard:** IFMI (International Financial Modeling Institute)
**Objective:** Validates advanced proficiency in spreadsheet-based financial modeling, valuation, and forecasting.

**Syllabus Modules:**
- **Forecasting Methodology:** Techniques for predicting future financial trends.
- **Financial Statement Analysis & Modeling:** Deep dive into account analysis and modeling.
- **Assumptions:** Setting macroeconomic and company-specific assumptions.
- **Model Design:** Preparation and structure of a robust financial model.
- **Subsidiaries Modeling:** Handling complex organizational structures.
- **Equity Valuation:** Application of various valuation methodologies.
- **Project Finance:** Project financial model and detailed analysis.
- **Risk & Analytics:** Financial analytics and risk analysis.
- **Credit Analysis:** Assessing creditworthiness and capacity.

**Practical Application:** Includes a "PFM Case Book" and a final exam requiring the construction of a full corporate financial model.

---

### 2. Financial Risk Analyst for Corporation (FRAC)
**Standard:** IFMI / PT Pendidikan Profesi Keuangan
**Objective:** Expertise in identifying, analyzing, and managing financial risks within a non-banking corporate environment.
**Focus:** Prerequisite training for the FRAC certification exam, focusing on corporate financial risk mitigation.

---

### 3. Professional Project Analyst (PPA)
**Standard:** IFMI
**Objective:** Validates competence in project feasibility, investment evaluation, and performance analysis.
**Key Focus:** Budgeting, forecasting, and strategic decision-making for large-scale projects.

---

### 4. Certified Risk Professional (CRP)
**Standard:** BNSP (Badan Nasional Sertifikasi Profesi) - National Standard
**Duration:** 5 Days (4 days training + 1 day competency test).
**Syllabus Highlights:**
- **ISO 31000:** Development of risk management standards.
- **Risk Architecture:** Designing a company's risk framework.
- **Risk Register:** Identification, depreciation, and recording of risks.
- **Treatment:** Planning and executing risk mitigation techniques.
- **Monitoring:** Risk dashboards and based decision making.
