# 🎓 IFMI Deep Dive: Certification Standards & Guidelines

This document provides a comprehensive breakdown of the International Financial Modeling Institute (IFMI) certification programs, their standards, and the process for obtaining credentials.

---

## 🌐 Institutional Framework
- **Role:** Independent Global Certification Body.
- **Compliance:** Aligned with **ISO/IEC 17024**, ensuring impartiality, transparency, and international recognition of certifications.
- **Operational Model:** IFMI acts as the validator (certification body) and partners with **Registered Training Providers (RTP)**, such as PT Rajawali Konsulindo, to deliver the actual education and training.

---

## 📜 Detailed Program Breakdown

### 1. Professional Financial Modeler (PFM)
**Focus:** Advanced spreadsheet-based financial modeling, valuation, and forecasting.

- **Body of Knowledge (BoK):**
    - Integration of accounting, statistics, economics, and financial mathematics.
    - Construction of dynamic models to simulate real-world business scenarios.
- **Exam Guidelines:**
    - **Flexibility:** Can be taken via independent study or formal courses.
    - **Format:** Paper-based, computer-based, or a hybrid. Online examinations are available via a secure platform.
    - **Requirements:** Registration requires a CV, professional photo, and government ID.
    - **Certification:** Issued as an e-certificate upon completion of all modules.

### 2. Professional Project Analyst (PPA)
**Focus:** Project budgeting, forecasting, and end-to-end feasibility analysis.

- **Competencies:**
    - Investment evaluation and financial modeling.
    - Risk assessment and strategic decision-making.
    - Integration of **AI-enhanced inputs** into feasibility models.
- **Examination Structure (Single-Level):**
    - **Module 1:** Multiple-choice assessment (Theoretical/Conceptual).
    - **Module 2:** Case Study Essay (Practical application of feasibility analysis).
- **Outcome:** PPA designation granted after passing both modules.

### 3. Financial Risk Analyst for Corporations (FRAC)
**Focus:** Managing financial risk specifically for **non-banking corporations**.

- **Objective:** Equipping risk managers with tools to handle liquidity, market, and credit risks within a corporate setting.
- **Standard:** Built on rigorous standards similar to PFM and PPA, with a dedicated Body of Knowledge.

---

## 🛠 Certification Journey (Typical Flow)

1. **Application:** Create account $\rightarrow$ Submit credentials (CV, ID).
2. **Preparation:** Study via official BoK $\rightarrow$ Training via RTP (e.g., RJ Consulting).
3. **Examination:** Take Module exams $\rightarrow$ Complete Case Studies.
4. **Validation:** IFMI reviews results $\rightarrow$ Issues Certification.
